**10篇閱讀理解答案**

### Jobs

**Passage 1**  
1. Tom wants to be a pilot.  
2. So that they can fly the plane safely. / Because they need to fly the plane safely.  
3. He will travel to many countries and wear a smart uniform.  
4. Tom’s uncle.

**Passage 2**  
1. Sarah’s dream job is to be a social worker. / She wants to be a social worker.  
2. Patient and understanding.  
3. She will talk to people and try to solve their problems.  
4. Because she wants to make the world better. / Because she likes helping people.

### 形容人物

**Passage 3**  
1. He is tall and good-looking.  
2. Outgoing and hard-working. (或 kind / caring 也可)  
3. Because he is kind and caring. / Because he always helps his classmates.  
4. A good teacher. / He will be a good teacher.

**Passage 4**  
1. She is the class teacher. / She is our class teacher.  
2. She never gets angry. She explains the answers carefully.  
3. Because she is patient, understanding and creative. / Because she thinks of fun ways to teach English.  
4. Patient, understanding / creative. (任何兩個)

### Action in films

**Passage 5**  
1. A strong robot. / He turns into a strong robot.  
2. He gets away quickly.  
3. The secret of the evil plan.  
4. No, he does not give up.

**Passage 6**  
1. Her lost brother.  
2. A clever plan.  
3. No, she doesn’t. / No, she never runs away.  
4. She finds her brother and they go home happily.

### 其他類型（較難）

**Passage 7**  
1. They help people.  
2. Strong and brave.  
3. They catch thieves and keep the streets safe.  
4. Yes, they are.

**Passage 8**  
1. YouTubers.  
2. Because they think it is an easy and fun job.  
3. Make interesting videos every week and answer a lot of comments.  
4. People will stop watching them.

**Passage 9**  
1. He helped his mum clean the house.  
2. The library. / He went to the library.  
3. One hour. / For one hour.  
4. He felt tired but happy because he finished everything.
