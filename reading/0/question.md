

### 一、閱讀理解 10 篇

#### Jobs（2篇）

**Passage 1**  
Tom wants to be a pilot when he grows up. He loves aeroplanes and the sky. Pilots need to be careful and calm so that they can fly the plane safely. If Tom becomes a pilot, he will travel to many countries. He will also wear a smart uniform. Tom’s uncle is a pilot. He often tells Tom interesting stories about flying.

Questions:  
1. What does Tom want to be?  
2. Why do pilots need to be careful and calm?  
3. What will Tom do if he becomes a pilot? (two things)  
4. Who is a pilot in Tom’s family?  

**Passage 2**  
Sarah’s dream job is to be a social worker. She likes helping people who are in trouble. Social workers need to be patient and understanding. When Sarah grows up, she will talk to people and try to solve their problems. She knows the job is not easy, but she still wants to do it because she wants to make the world better.

Questions:  
1. What is Sarah’s dream job?  
2. What do social workers need to be like?  
3. What will Sarah do when she grows up?  
4. Why does Sarah want to be a social worker?

#### 形容人物（2篇）

**Passage 3**  
My best friend is called Leo. He is tall and good-looking. Leo is also very outgoing and hard-working. He always helps his classmates when they have problems. Everyone likes him because he is kind and caring. I think Leo will be a good teacher in the future.

Questions:  
1. What does Leo look like?  
2. What kind of person is Leo? (two adjectives)  
3. Why do people like Leo?  
4. What does the writer think Leo will be?

**Passage 4**  
Miss Chan is our class teacher. She is patient and understanding. When we make mistakes, she never gets angry. Instead, she explains the answers carefully. Miss Chan is also creative. She always thinks of fun ways to teach us English. We all love her very much.

Questions:  
1. Who is Miss Chan?  
2. What does Miss Chan do when students make mistakes?  
3. Why do the students love Miss Chan?  
4. Find two adjectives that describe Miss Chan.

#### Action in films（2篇）

**Passage 5**  
In the film, the hero turns into a strong robot. The bad people try to catch him, but he gets away quickly. Later, he finds out the secret of the evil plan. At the end, he does not give up and saves the city. The film is very exciting.

Questions:  
1. What does the hero turn into?  
2. What happens when the bad people try to catch him?  
3. What does the hero find out?  
4. Does the hero give up at the end?

**Passage 6**  
Lily watched an action film last night. In the story, the girl looks for her lost brother. She makes up a clever plan and sets off on a long journey. On the way, she gets into trouble many times, but she never runs away. Finally, she finds her brother and they go home happily.

Questions:  
1. What does the girl look for?  
2. What does she make up?  
3. Does she run away when she gets into trouble?  
4. How does the story end?

#### 其他類型（較難）3篇

**Passage 7** (比較型)  
Both fire fighters and policemen help people, but their jobs are different. Fire fighters put out fires and save people from burning buildings. They need to be strong and brave. Policemen catch thieves and keep the streets safe. They need to be careful and quick. Both jobs are dangerous, but they are very important to us.

Questions:  
1. What do both fire fighters and policemen do?  
2. What do fire fighters need to be like?  
3. What do policemen do?  
4. Are the two jobs dangerous?

**Passage 8** (因果 + 觀點)  
Many children want to be YouTubers nowadays. They think it is an easy and fun job. However, being a YouTuber is not as easy as they think. YouTubers need to make interesting videos every week. They also need to answer a lot of comments. If they cannot keep making good videos, people will stop watching them.

Questions:  
1. What do many children want to be?  
2. Why do they think it is a good job?  
3. What do YouTubers need to do? (two things)  
4. What will happen if they cannot make good videos?

**Passage 9** (時間順序 + 細節)  
Yesterday was a busy day for Ken. In the morning, he helped his mum clean the house. After lunch, he did his homework and then went to the library. In the evening, he practised the piano for one hour. Before he went to bed, he read a storybook. Ken felt tired but happy because he finished everything.

Questions:  
1. What did Ken do in the morning?  
2. Where did Ken go after he finished his homework?  
3. How long did Ken practise the piano?  
4. How did Ken feel at the end of the day? Why?
