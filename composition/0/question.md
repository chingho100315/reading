### 二、作文題 3 篇（對應三個課題）

**Composition 1 – Jobs**  
Topic: My Dream Job  
Write about 60–80 words.  
You may use these questions:  
- What do you want to be when you grow up?  
- Why do you want to do this job?  
- What will you do if you become this person?

**Composition 2 – Describing People**  
Topic: My Favourite Person  
Write about 60–80 words.  
You may write about:  
- Who is this person?  
- What does he/she look like?  
- What kind of person is he/she? (use adjectives)  
- Why do you like him/her?

**Composition 3 – Actions in Films**  
Topic: A Film I Watched  
Write about 60–80 words.  
You may write about:  
- What is the name of the film?  
- What happens in the film? (use action verbs: turn into, get away, find out, give up…)  
- Do you like the film? Why?
